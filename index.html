Privacy Policy

Glow & Grace Salon FAQ Chatbot

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. About this chatbot

This chatbot is a demonstration project designed to answer frequently asked questions about a fictional hair salon called Glow & Grace Salon. It is not an official booking or payment service.

2. Information processed

When you message the chatbot through WhatsApp, your phone number, message content, and message-related technical information may be processed to receive your message and generate a reply.

3. How information is used

Information is processed to respond to questions using the salon's FAQ information, operate the chatbot, and troubleshoot technical issues. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information.

4. Third-party services

WhatsApp and Meta may process messages and related information when you use their services. The chatbot also uses Groq's AI service to generate responses. Information sent to these providers is subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

  • WhatsApp Privacy Policy
  • Groq Privacy Policy

5. Storage and retention

This demonstration is not designed as a customer database. However, messages or technical information may be retained by service providers or appear in operational logs, depending on their configurations and policies. We do not promise immediate or automatic deletion.

6. Data security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect access credentials and application data. As this is a demonstration project, please avoid sharing confidential or sensitive information with it.

7. Your choices and requests

You can stop using the chatbot at any time. For questions about this demonstration or requests concerning information handled by its operator, contact the project maintainer through the GitHub profile. Requests involving WhatsApp, Meta, or Groq may also need to be directed to those providers.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the chatbot's functionality or data handling changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
